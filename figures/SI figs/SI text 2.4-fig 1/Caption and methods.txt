Calibrated concentrations of seven PFAS in aquatic foods, 2003–2016. Boxes span the 25th–75th percentiles and horizontal lines mark the median. Concentrations are expressed in ng/g wet weight. The country- and year-specific marginal concentration pools were inputs to the U.S. dietary estimated daily intake simulation. Draws for each food and congener were allocated among producers according to their shares of food-specific production in that year, renormalized over producers with an available pool. Marine fish combine demersal, pelagic and other marine fish. Other seafood combines crustaceans and molluscs. Food types within each group were sampled in proportion to production. Within food types, Σ7PFAS was reconstructed by independently sampling and summing the seven marginal distributions. The source files sort each congener independently, so the original sample-level co-occurrence is unavailable. The reconstructed sum pools origins separately for each congener. Each annual food-group distribution uses 250,000 draws. Production coverage varies by food, congener and year. For example, available pools for PFHxS, PFNA, PFDA and PFUnDA covered 0.9% of production in the other marine fish subgroup in 2003. The accompanying CSV provides coverage for each input.

The input pools are in water_conc year 2003 through water_conc year 2016 in US 2003-2016 time depend Mix_k. The weights use the Production records from Sheet1 of summary of supply qunatity -2009.xlsx (2003–2009) and raw of summary of supply qunatity 2010-.xlsx (2010–2016).
