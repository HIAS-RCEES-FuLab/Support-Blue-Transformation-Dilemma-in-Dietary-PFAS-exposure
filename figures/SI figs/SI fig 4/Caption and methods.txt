Estimated versus measured serum concentrations of Σ4PFAS, PFOS and PFOA. The estimated values are geometric means. Measured values are the source-reported statistics selected in the project workbook. Markers identify measured cohort type. Dotted grey lines show equality. Dashed coloured lines show least-squares fits on log10-transformed concentrations. Pearson r, two-sided p and R² are from the log10-scale analyses. This figure reproduces the comparisons in main Fig. 2c–e.
