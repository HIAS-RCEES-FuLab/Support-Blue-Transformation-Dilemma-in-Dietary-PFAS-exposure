Projected growth in real GDP per capita and changes in the dietary PFAS hazard index, 2020–2050. Each dot represents one of 47 countries. Dashed lines show ordinary least-squares fits. Panels show Business as Usual and High Road in 2050 Metatrade mode relative to 2020 Trade. Real GDP per capita growth is 100 × [(GDPV2050 / POP2050) / (GDPV2020 / POP2020) − 1]. GDPV is the OECD Economic Outlook No. 114 long-term baseline GDP volume series in national currency. The within-country ratio cancels currency units. Population estimates and projections are from the project copy of WorldBank population projection -2050.xlsx. HI change is 100 × (HI2050 / HI2020 − 1), using the full-precision country means underlying SI Table 2. Pearson correlations and two-sided p values use the unweighted country pairs.
