SI Fig. 6 | Modeled serum PFOS and PFOA concentrations and source composition. a, The coloured and grey lines show the sum of the arithmetic mean PFOS and PFOA serum concentrations estimated by physiologically based kinetic modelling for each two-year period from 2003 to 2016. The three scenarios are no exposure after 2004, dietary exposure from terrestrial foods only, and dietary exposure from terrestrial and aquatic foods. The black line shows the corresponding NHANES means. Black vertical bars span the 25th to 75th percentiles of measured combined concentrations. For the terrestrial and aquatic food scenario, the red band is bounded by the sums of the compound-specific 25th and 75th percentiles, respectively. b, Percentage of the combined-food scenario mean attributed to each source by differencing the modeled scenarios. Carryover from 2004 is the no-exposure-after-2004 estimate. The terrestrial contribution is the terrestrial-only estimate minus carryover. The aquatic contribution is the combined-food estimate minus the terrestrial-only estimate. The 2003–2004 cycle is the common calibration period and is omitted from b. Model calibration is described in SI Text 11.
