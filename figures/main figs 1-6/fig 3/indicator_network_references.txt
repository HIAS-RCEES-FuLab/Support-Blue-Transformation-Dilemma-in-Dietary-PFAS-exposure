FIGURE 3D INDICATOR NETWORK REFERENCES
15 November 2026

1. Haemoglobin and total bilirubin
   Pimstone, N. R., Tenhunen, R., Seitz, P. T., Marver, H. S. & Schmid, R.
   The enzymatic degradation of hemoglobin to bile pigments by macrophages.
   J. Exp. Med. 133, 1264–1281 (1971).
   https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.133.6.1264
   This biochemical link concerns haem breakdown and bilirubin formation.
   SI Text 8 also discusses haem breakdown.

2. Serum albumin and total bilirubin
   Jacobsen, J. & Brodersen, R. Albumin-bilirubin binding mechanism.
   J. Biol. Chem. 258, 6319–6326 (1983).
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6853485/
   Albumin binds unconjugated bilirubin. The model indicator is total bilirubin,
   which includes this fraction. The edge describes binding.

3. Serum albumin and total calcium
   Held, I. R. & Freeman, S. Binding of calcium by human plasma proteins under
   simulated physiologic conditions. J. Appl. Physiol. 19, 292–296 (1964).
   https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.1964.19.2.292
   Total calcium includes calcium bound to albumin. The edge describes binding.

4. Uric acid and hypertension
   Sundström, J. et al. Relations of serum uric acid to longitudinal blood
   pressure tracking and hypertension incidence. Hypertension 45, 28–33 (2005).
   https://doi.org/10.1161/01.HYP.0000150784.92944.9a
   The Framingham cohort found a prospective association with new hypertension.
   The modeled NHANES hypertension variable records a history of diagnosis.

5. Gamma-glutamyltransferase (GGT) and hypertension
   Lee, D.-H. et al. Gamma-glutamyltransferase is a predictor of incident
   diabetes and hypertension: the CARDIA Study. Clin. Chem. 49, 1358–1366 (2003).
   https://doi.org/10.1373/49.8.1358
   CARDIA reported a prospective association with incident hypertension.
   The modeled NHANES hypertension variable records a history of diagnosis.

6. Systolic and diastolic blood pressure and hypertension
   Whelton, P. K. et al. 2017 ACC/AHA guideline for the prevention, detection,
   evaluation, and management of high blood pressure in adults.
   Circulation 138, e484–e594 (2018).
   https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0000000000000596
   National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey blood pressure questionnaire.
   https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhanes/public/2013/questionnaires/BPQ_H.pdf
   These edges show the clinical relationship between BP measurements and
   hypertension. BPQ020 records whether a health professional ever told the
   participant that they had hypertension.

7. Total cholesterol and hypertension
   Sesso, H. D. et al. A prospective study of plasma lipid levels and hypertension
   in women. Arch. Intern. Med. 165, 2420–2427 (2005).
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16287773/
   Higher total cholesterol was associated with higher incident hypertension
   in this cohort of women. This edge uses evidence external to SI Text 8.

8. Serum GGT and oxidative stress
   Lee, D.-H. & Jacobs, D. R. Jr. Is serum gamma glutamyltransferase a marker
   of oxidative stress? Free Radic. Res. 38, 535–539 (2004).
   https://doi.org/10.1080/10715760410001694026
   SI Text 8 ref. 81. Serum GGT is a proposed, nonspecific marker.

9. Disturbed blood flow and oxidative stress
    De Keulenaer, G. W. et al. Oscillatory and steady laminar shear stress
    differentially affect human endothelial redox state. Circ. Res. 82,
    1094–1101 (1998). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9622162/
    Endothelial cells exposed to oscillatory shear generated superoxide.
    The context is vascular oxidative stress under disturbed flow.

10. Oxidative stress and arthritis subtypes
    Seven, A. et al. Lipid, protein, DNA oxidation and antioxidant status in
    rheumatoid arthritis. Clin. Biochem. 41, 538–543 (2008).
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinbiochem.2008.01.029
    Supporting study in osteoarthritis and other arthritic conditions:
    Protein oxidation, nitration and glycation biomarkers for early-stage
    diagnosis of osteoarthritis of the knee and typing and progression of
    arthritic disease. Arthritis Res. Ther. (2016).
    https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27788684/
    The edge refers to observations in specific arthritic conditions.
    Arthritis is the broader modelled outcome.

11. Serum urate and gout
    Campion, E. W., Glynn, R. J. & DeLabry, L. O. Asymptomatic hyperuricemia.
    Risks and consequences in the Normative Aging Study. Am. J. Med. 82,
    421–426 (1987). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3826098/
    Higher serum urate was prospectively associated with incident gout.

12. Serum creatinine and kidney function
    Levey, A. S. et al. A new equation to estimate glomerular filtration rate.
    Ann. Intern. Med. 150, 604–612 (2009).
    https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19414839/
    Serum creatinine is an input to creatinine-based eGFR. A modelled increase
    in creatinine on its own is insufficient to establish kidney dysfunction.

13. Bicarbonate and kidney function
    de Brito-Ashurst, I. et al. Bicarbonate supplementation slows progression
    of CKD and improves nutritional status. J. Am. Soc. Nephrol. 20,
    2075–2084 (2009). https://doi.org/10.1681/ASN.2008111205
    SI Text 8 includes bicarbonate as a renal-related marker. This trial concerns
    metabolic acidosis in established CKD. Bicarbonate is shown as context.

14. Total calcium and kidney function
    Ketteler, M. et al. Executive summary of the 2017 KDIGO CKD–MBD guideline
    update. Kidney Int. 92, 26–36 (2017).
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.kint.2017.04.006
    Kidney disease can alter mineral metabolism. Total calcium has other
    determinants, including protein binding. The edge is clinical context.

15. Serum urate and kidney function
    Jain, R. B. & Ducatman, A. Dynamics of associations between perfluoroalkyl
    substances and uric acid across the various stages of glomerular function.
    Environ. Sci. Pollut. Res. 26, 12425–12434 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-019-04666-5
    This SI Text 8 citation examines how associations with serum urate vary
    across kidney function. The edge indicates renal context.

16. PPAR-α and lipid metabolism
    Kersten, S. et al. Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor α mediates
    the adaptive response to fasting. J. Clin. Invest. 103, 1489–1498 (1999).
    https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10359558/
    Mechanistic work in mice connects PPAR-α with hepatic fatty acid oxidation.
    The diagram does not assign the modelled cholesterol change to PPAR-α.

17. Total cholesterol and lipid metabolism
    The total cholesterol node represents a circulating lipid measure. See the
    clinical interpretations of lipid levels in the NHLBI lipid panel.
    https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/blood-cholesterol/diagnosis
    This contextual edge groups the biomarker with the physiological process.

18. PFAS – serum albumin
    Forsthuber, M. et al. Albumin is the major carrier protein for PFOS,
    PFOA, PFHxS, PFNA and PFDA in human plasma. Environ. Int. 137, 105324
    (2020). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envint.2019.105324
    SI Text 8 ref. 76. Albumin was the predominant carrier for these five
    congeners in the human plasma samples examined.

19. PFAS – haemoglobin
    Trousdale, R. C., Tokmina-Lukaszewska, M., Bothner, B. & Walker, R. A.
    Perfluorooctanoic acid destabilizes hemoglobin structure at
    submicromolar concentrations. J. Phys. Chem. B 129, 5217–5223 (2025).
    https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.5c01380
    SI Text 8 ref. 77. Native mass spectrometry and spectroscopy showed
    interaction with human haemoglobin in vitro. This edge applies to PFOA.

20. PFAS – gamma-globulins
    Fischer, F. C. et al. Binding of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances
    (PFAS) to serum proteins: implications for toxicokinetics in humans.
    Environ. Sci. Technol. (2024). https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.3c07415
    SI Text 8 ref. 78. In vitro binding was assessed with purified human
    gamma-globulins as a representative globulin fraction. Binding varied
    among the compounds tested.

21. PFAS – PPAR-α
    Khazaee, M. et al. Perfluoroalkyl acid binding with peroxisome
    proliferator-activated receptors α, γ, and δ, and fatty acid binding
    proteins by equilibrium dialysis with a comparison of methods.
    Toxics 9, 45 (2021). https://doi.org/10.3390/toxics9030045
    SI Text 8 ref. 74, Table 3. PFHxA and PFNA bound to PPAR-α in equilibrium
    dialysis experiments. This is receptor binding evidence.

22. PFAS – renal organic anion transporter 3 (OAT3)
    Ryu, S. et al. Evaluation of 14 PFAS for permeability and organic anion
    transporter interactions: implications for renal clearance in humans.
    Chemosphere 361, 142390 (2024).
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemosphere.2024.142390
    SI Text 8 ref. 84, Fig. 3B. Cell assays identified several substrates
    of human OAT3, including PFOA, PFNA and PFHxS from the study's target
    congeners. The edge represents transport activity. It provides no
    inference about altered creatinine or serum urate in the model.

23. Gout and other arthritis subtypes
    Jebakumar, A. J., Udayakumar, P. D., Crowson, C. S. & Matteson, E. L.
    Occurrence of gout in rheumatoid arthritis: it does happen! A
    population-based study. Int. J. Clin. Rheumatol. 8, 433–437 (2013).
    https://doi.org/10.2217/ijr.13.45
    Kang, S. et al. Risk of incident gout in rheumatoid arthritis from a
    nationwide cohort study in South Korea. Sci. Rep. 15, 26970 (2025).
    https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-12696-y
    Roddy, E., Zhang, W. & Doherty, M. Are joints affected by gout also
    affected by osteoarthritis? Ann. Rheum. Dis. 66, 1374–1377 (2007).
    https://doi.org/10.1136/ard.2006.063768
    The cohort studies concern rheumatoid arthritis, and the joint-level
    study concerns osteoarthritis. The line denotes clinical literature
    on specific subtypes. NHANES asks about arthritis (MCQ160A) and gout
    (MCQ160N) in separate items.
    https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhanes/public/2013/questionnaires/MCQ_H.pdf

24. Albumin and the albumin/globulin ratio
    The study defines C_AGR as serum albumin (LBXSAL) divided by total
    serum globulin (LBXSGB). This edge represents a calculation input.
    The gamma-globulins node refers to the narrower protein fraction
    used in the PFAS binding study.
    Source: SI Table 4 and the archived project calculation in
    respond/code avalibility/BlueFood Portable Pipeline/research/health.py.
    The Fig. 3c source workbook (sheet "to plot") gives 96.69% direction
    agreement for an increase in the ratio in the 2020 modelled population.

25. Total cholesterol and LDL-cholesterol
    SI Tables 4 and 5 and the Fig. 3c source workbook report LDL-cholesterol
    under the study-specific code C_LDL. The archived implementation
    calculates C_LDL as LBXSCH / 18 minus LBDHDDSI. LBXSCH is serum
    total cholesterol and LBDHDDSI is the HDL-cholesterol input. The
    edge represents an input to the reported calculation.
    Source: respond/code avalibility/BlueFood Portable Pipeline/research/health.py.
    The standard NHANES LDL-cholesterol variable uses a different calculation
    that includes triglycerides:
    https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2015/DataFiles/TRIGLY_I.htm

Suggested caption

d, Relationships among selected health indicators and outcomes, grouped
by physiological domain. Smaller italic labels mark biological processes,
molecular targets and contextual diagnoses. Lines from PFAS represent
binding or transporter activity for the compounds tested. Arthritis denotes
the modelled NHANES outcome for reporting a prior professional diagnosis
(MCQ160A). Its connections to oxidative stress and gout draw on published
studies of specific arthritis subtypes. Hypertension denotes reported
prior diagnosis (BPQ020). The albumin-to-ratio link identifies a calculation input. The total-to-LDL-cholesterol link represents an input to the study's reported C_LDL calculation. Circle size
and line weight are layout features
and do not represent effect sizes.
